    How it runs

    Twenty-four problems, released in three themed sets of eight, across one 12-hour clock. Set A is JEE and pre-college life, Set B is film, music and internet culture, Set C is campus life. Every statement ends with a “Learn More”, that's where you should spend more time

    Scoring

    Base points come from a problem's set and slot (100-600). Each wrong submission decays the value by 15%, floored at 40% *and compile errors never count, so experiment freely. The first three solvers of any problem take a 1.20 / 1.12 / 1.06 bonus. Ties break on fewest wrong submissions, then earliest last solve.

    On pace All 24 problems are out within the 12-hour window. Scoring does not reward grinding non-stop, so take breaks, step away, and come back fresh.

    Prizes

    Three overall, best-in-set for A/B/C, and a long bench of recognition awards - Certified Wrong Answer Machine, One Shot One Kill, The Comeback, Lone Wolf, Code Golf Champion, and more. The recognition awards carry the majority of the prize count, because most of whats worth celebrating the leaderboard cant see.

    Rules & conduct

    TL;DR : play fair, be kind, and ask in the open.

    1. Individual participation. One account per person; multiple accounts are grounds for disqualification.
    2. No sharing code, approaches, or hints while the contest is live. Discussion opens after T+12h.
    3. Pre-written personal templates and reference material are allowed.
    4. AI is permitted only for concept explanation. Requesting or submitting a full solution to a contest problem is not.
    5. Organizers dont compete for prizes. Seniors may play for fun; their scores show separately.
    6. Clarifications go through the announcement channel only - answers are broadcast to everyone.
    7. Report suspected violations privately to a sub-coordinator. Reports are confidential and reviewed by two organizers.
    8. Harassment or targeting of any participant means immediate removal.
    9. Organizer decisions on scoring are final, but every dispute is logged and its resolution published.
